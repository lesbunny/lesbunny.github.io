Local Edit
Remastered

Change how another person's message appears on your device. Your local edits are saved by the plugin and are not sent to Discord or shown to other people.

Install

  1. Open Discord in a compatible Revenge, Kettu, or Vendetta-based client.
  2. Go to the client's Plugins page.
  3. Copy the plugin URL and paste it into the plugin installer.
  4. Long-press another person's message and choose Edit Locally.
